Directional / CrossTrade quote integration (2026-10-21)

Scope
The existing Directional strategy and CrossTrade native LIMIT transport are
unchanged. A/B remains shadow. No new service, strategy or real-money test.
Quote monitoring is not order authorization. No candle, last or midpoint fallback.

Quote adapter
DIRECTIONAL_QUOTE_MONITOR_ENABLED=true enables the in-process GET-only monitor
at 30-second intervals. /execution/quotes returns its most recent persisted
observation without another provider request. /execution/readiness includes
quote provenance and observation age. Original response text and parsed data,
request latency, provider timestamps and validation decision are stored in
DATA_DIR/directional_quotes.sqlite3; retention 30 days / 100,000 rows.
The endpoint is fixed, redirects disabled, timeout 5 seconds, body <=64KiB.
Exact dated MNQ contract, tick/value metadata, positive tick-aligned bid <= ask,
provider timestamps, freshness and completeness are checked. Collective uses
the older of ts/eventTs for diagnostic age only; their semantics are unverified.
confidence=high never authorizes an order. collective remains monitoring-only.

Documented NT8 Live snapshots may pass the existing execution adapter with
DIRECTIONAL_QUOTE_SOURCE=crosstrade_nt8 and an approved execution policy.
There is no flag that promotes collective to executable. Approval of parameters
cannot override explicit execution_eligible=false. Final execution fetches a
new quote; the monitor cache is never submitted as execution evidence.

Quote traffic is paced to at most one request per 2 seconds across local
processes sharing DATA_DIR, without automatic retries or waiting in an entry
call. 429 Retry-After (minimum 7-second backoff), transport/5xx backoff and
pacing survive process restart. CrossTrade's 3 requests/second budget is shared
across ALL clients of the user; the quote limiter does not govern unrelated
external applications. Monitor traffic is just 2 requests/minute. A rate limit
or unavailable quote fails entry closed. The 10-second monitor age ceiling is
a diagnostic classification, not an approved executable freshness threshold.

Final sequence
Exact setup AI ALLOW and original geometry are bound to the same order.
The existing native reservation is persisted before final validation. Slow
account and thesis reads precede the fresh quote. The final pure gate then
checks price/limit, spread, R:R including costs, account/thesis/review ages and
identity. Native transport again checks kill switch, expiry, ownership and
cancellation immediately before POST. Original LIMIT and bracket are unchanged;
no conversion to MARKET. Broker ACK is not fill or proof of protection.

Risk repair
Actual Tradovate USD/SOD balance field aliases are normalized. Missing or
conflicting balances block. Provider allowance proof remains account-bound,
dated, and mandatory. No panel floor or approved parameter is fabricated.

External evidence still required
- CrossTrade's definition of collective bid/ask: actual unaveraged CME BBO or
  aggregate/indicative prices, timestamp semantics and update/staleness policy.
  An independent executable feed comparison where accessible. Public docs:
  https://crosstrade.io/docs/api/market/get-quote
  https://crosstrade.io/docs/api/rate-limiting
- Current MFFU stage/plan option, absolute floor, daily allowance, contract cap
  and validity period from the specific account. User supplied floor 48161.90
  and balance 48721.05; this is not a complete dated provider allowance proof.
- Representative NYAM/NYPM quotes, exact AI durations and actual fill evidence
  for approved spread/age/entry-zone/volatility/slippage parameters. A short
  overnight sample cannot validate NY execution thresholds or probable slippage.
- Provider paper account for independent broker-side certification. MFFU Builder
  demo is not used as a disposable paper account. Local protocol simulator is
  explicitly labelled, and does not certify external protection behavior.

Fixed gross 2R incompatibility
For gross stop risk L, target G, round-trip costs C and stop stress S,
net RR=(G-C)/(L+C+S). >=2 requires G>=2L+3C+2S. Fixed G=2L cannot pass with
positive costs. Do not silently move TP/entry or approve a new bracket policy.
The existing 3R runner requires its own acceptance checks and user approval.

Controlled activation (ONLY after all acceptance checks and explicit approval)
1. Keep LIVE_EXECUTION_ENABLED=false, EXEC_KILL_SWITCH=true, Guard OFF,
   AUTO_SUBMIT=0, EXEC_MODE=off and V3_ENTRY_ARMED=0 during verification.
2. Supply reviewed DIRECTIONAL_EXECUTION_POLICY_FILE and current
   MFFU_RISK_EVIDENCE_FILE. Verify provider provenance and dated contract,
   fees, current account-wide inventory/P&L, calendar, sessions, entry geometry,
   net >=2R and broker lifecycle/protection. Freeze hashes and release SHA.
3. Obtain explicit activation approval. Keep EXEC_STRATEGY_POLICY=V3_ONLY,
   AB_ENTRY_ARMED=0, AB_MANAGER_MODE=shadow, BUILDER_ORDER_PROVIDER=crosstrade,
   AB_AI_ENABLED=true, AB_AI_OWNER=AB_DIRECTIONAL, AB_AI_TRIGGER=SETUP,
   V3_AI_REVIEW_REQUIRED=true, AB_AI_MAX_CALLS_PER_DAY=12. Keep NYAM 09:30-11:00
   and NYPM 13:30-16:00 America/New_York. The call cap is not a dollar budget.
4. The entry switches, after checks, are AB_V3_MODE=LIVE,
   AB_DIRECTIONAL_LIVE_LONG=1, AB_DIRECTIONAL_LIVE_SHORT=1, V3_ENTRY_ARMED=1,
   AUTO_SUBMIT=1, EXEC_MODE=auto, LIVE_EXECUTION_ENABLED=true and Guard AUTO.
   Keep kill true until effective configuration/protection is verified, then
   user-authorized release of EXEC_KILL_SWITCH=false. Do not replay history.
   V3_EXIT_MODE=real / V3_EXIT_REAL_EXECUTION=true require existing ownership/
   parity checks; V3_RUNNER_ENABLED remains false until separately accepted.
   Preserve the currently tighter DAY_LOSS_N=1 (not the default 2),
   DAY_LOSS_USD=550, MAX_TRADES_DAY=3, EXEC_MAX_QTY=15,
   AB_DIRECTIONAL_RISK_USD_50K=250 and rolling LOSS_STREAK_N=3.
5. Observe native ACK, actual fills and matching working stop/target/OCO,
   not merely SENT/HTTP 200. Uncertainty blocks further entries.

Rollback
First set EXEC_KILL_SWITCH=true and AUTO_SUBMIT=0 / Guard OFF to stop entries.
If exposure exists, keep the master and protective-exit path available; disabling
all writes would also disable necessary cancellation/close operations. Confirm
broker-side protection and resolve outstanding positions/orders manually if
needed. Only when flat with zero working orders set LIVE_EXECUTION_ENABLED=false,
V3_ENTRY_ARMED=0 and Directional flags off. Redeploy the last verified release
with these disabled variables, preserving /data journals and idempotency keys.
Verify SHA, health, positions, orders and restart recovery. Never delete state.
