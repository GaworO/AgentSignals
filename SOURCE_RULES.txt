ENTRY UPDATE — USER APPROVED OCTOBER 13
Both new families work in either direction:
1. Manipulation / liquidity event -> selected swing break -> selected-zone
   retracement -> strict M1 inversion close. The swing break has an explicit
   role: directional break OR liquidity sweep. In particular, a low sweep can
   precede a long; its bullish M1 inversion supplies the entry confirmation.
2. Existing H1/H4 objective touched -> directional M15 displacement / FVG ->
   retracement into that gap -> strict M1 inversion close. This also supports
   shorts. No universal M5 confirmation is added to either family.

All profiles, including selected_inversion and m5_momentum, support immediate
confirmation entry or optional further retracement at a selected MNQ price.
Retracement must improve on the MNQ confirmation close. Pending entries expire
and cancel on invalidation, final target or news blackout. They never acquire a
historical fill from the trigger candle or an inferred OHLC path. Quote-touch
fills, acknowledgement and slippage remain explicit paper assumptions.

The AI schema now includes nullable entry_setup, explicit family/sequence,
assessment evidence, timing, entry anchor and optional local objective. Existing
v3 audits remain readable. The same scheduled call produces these selections;
no additional calls or spending cadence were introduced. entry_watch provides reusable context for each NEW inversion. Older complete
entry_setup selections can narrowly reuse their fixed choices within expiry.
Direction-only reviews cannot manufacture missing entry rules.

USER MANAGEMENT POLICY
An explicitly selected local objective uses floor(original FILLED quantity/3),
including any reduction for modeled costs. Skip when zero; preserve the runner.
Added lots do not change the original-position quantity. New source-mode paper
entries stop after two NET losing trades on the New York calendar date; net
break-even trades do not count. Broker/account-wide realized trade history is
not connected to this local simulator.

ATR exhaustion blocks NEW continuation entries only, including pending entries
before a fill. Open positions continue their selected management. The ATR guard
requires a fresh, complete, dated MNQ measurement with a stated convention.
daily_atr.py now calculates the measurement from stored closed MNQ candles,
using five completed full-session true ranges and today’s session range. Missing
history blocks continuations with an explicit reason; reversal setups retain
their usual checks. See CONTEXT_ATR_UPDATE.md for boundaries and overrides.

The previous user-custom M5/20% core is preserved for reproducibility with
TANJA_STRATEGY_POLICY=legacy_user_core, and PaperLifecycle(strategy_mode=
'legacy_user_core'). It is not a verified copy of Tanja. Existing saved events
cannot be silently reopened with a different policy: configuration mismatch
rejects the simulation. Use a new database for the new policy.

CHANGES
- Candidate discovery includes M1/M2/M3/M5 instead of universally enforcing M5.
  It still uses a limited geometry catalogue and engineering windows. Discovery
  is not complete coverage of all her discretionary setup families.
- M5 momentum is a separate profile requiring the selected swing close.
- Source plans require referenced context, confirmation-strength, timing,
  invalidation and target-priority assessments. Missing judgments do not inherit
  the proxy's nearest target or outermost reaction stop as her preferred choice.
- Partial quantities are explicit contracts per selected objective. There is no
  universal 20% default. ES must reach the selected objective; merely printing a
  new session extreme does not trigger the extra reduction.
- Trailing requires a selected swing, explicit buffer and confirmed recovery or
  break of a selected earlier extreme. Strict one-left/one-right plus one tick is
  confined to legacy mode, not presented as her universal rule.
- A source-mode simulator consumes recompiled source plans and records exact
  selected partials, stop requests, acknowledgements and bracket resizing.
  Filled/rejected/held acknowledgements remain supplied simulation assumptions.

ACCOUNT OVERLAY (user instructions, NOT reconstructed Tanja rules)
0.5% of current reported balance, cost-aware simulated sizing, shared risk,
account limits and no risk increase by crediting unrealized gains. The ±5-minute
news entry blackout and T-minus-60-second entry offset of 1.5 points remain.
These overlays intentionally can prevent trades or change exits shown in her
recordings. They therefore preclude literal account-behaviour equivalence.

TEACHING LIBRARY
decision_examples.json maps 15 choices from six previously studied recordings.
decision_library.py adds earlier-session examples to AIReview.make_payload;
library contents enter the API configuration fingerprint. Current-session and
later examples are excluded. Source paths remain local; prompt examples identify
the recording filename and video timestamp. No API request is made by building
the prompt. Existing daily limits and order separation remain in force.

These are curated qualitative annotations, NOT 15 aligned raw-market training
pairs. All six sessions influenced development, so none is an untouched holdout.
The underlying general prompt has also evolved from these sources; excluding
same-day examples alone does not establish clean out-of-sample performance.
No fine-tuning, paid model evaluation, or broker trading was performed here.

SOURCE REVIEW INTEGRATION
entry_review.py bridges saved, evidence-checked AI entry_setup to entry_rules.py;
SelectionObserver's normal background loop consumes it. Current account data
supplies sizing rather than the model. Response availability and current plan
selection are recorded separately; no fill predates the response. The explicit
source_review interface remains available for offline human-review fixtures.
Saved cutoff/revision records remain immutable. Dashboard candidates link to
the specific review/entry selection and show price, stop, target or the blocking
reason. Unreviewed snapshots are labelled as awaiting review.

STILL UNRESOLVED
Fidelity of model-selected setup, confirmation strength, invalidation and target;
exact catalyst/re-entry acceptance; staged add quantities
and separate added-lot exits; validation of the ATR convention; discretionary early
exits; and runner treatment beyond a selected target. Source mode does not call
the old one-add/20% function. Final target still closes the simulator's remainder;
it cannot reproduce the July 17 runner beyond target without another explicit
management variant. Short structural symmetry is tested mechanically, not proved
for every discretionary short example.
