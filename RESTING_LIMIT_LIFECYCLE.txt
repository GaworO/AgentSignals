Directional resting LIMIT lifecycle repair — engineering release, NOT activation
Base: 15c999f3c90dbad0e4599b6ef5e04d3464a123c2

Scope
Existing native CrossTrade/Tradovate adapter only. No new strategy, service,
subscription, market-price fallback, broker endpoint, or execution permission.
A/B remains shadow. The quote admission gate still blocks without its existing
approved executable-quote policy. Pending monitoring cannot authorize submission.

Repairs
A. Durable cancel attempts: reconcile exact account/contract/parent/clOrdId before
retry; 30-second transport backoff; process lock plus SQLite reservation; do not
retry PendingCancel or unresolved provider Cancel commands. A timeout/ACK is never
terminal. Retry only the same entry ID. Never create a replacement entry.
B. Persist a partial ACK's original entry ID; recover via clOrdId when ACK is lost.
Read parent first, cancel independently of unavailable child data. Children are
cancelled only while Suspended, on a terminal unfilled parent after a fresh
account/parent/fill recheck. Working SL/TP are never orphan-cleanup candidates. A filled
position's protection is not cancelled just to cancel its remaining entry.
C. Persist broker exposure before child reads. Native-verified late fills are
accepted as broker facts even after setup/AI expiry. No new setup approval is
created. Protected late fills remain under their original native bracket. Missing
protection queues the existing exact-owned-position recovery, never a blind
account flatten. Refresh stale account snapshots after slow child reads. Contradictory
account/contract/OCO evidence and malformed lifecycle packets still block writes.
D. Native-owned favorable fills are valid inside the original structural stop.
Record actual entry, stop distance/USD risk, target distance and gross RR. Preserve
the original approved limit, stop and target. Broker P&L uses actual executions.
E. Pending validator checks closed M1 freshness, original setup/geometry/BOS and
invalidation, same AI review/expiry, current risk evidence, account inventory,
quantity/risk cap, Guard/news/session and entry permissions. It cancels ineligible
remaining entries and continues reconciliation. Only independently owned pending
orders are excluded from prospective flat-account risk calculations; foreign
orders or any open exposure veto waiting. Partial remainder is conservatively
cancelled when the pending validator sees exposure; no protective resizing added.

Persistence and alerts
Additive native_order_phase and native_operator_alerts tables on existing SQLite.
PREPARED -> SUBMITTED -> ACKNOWLEDGED -> WORKING / PARTIALLY_FILLED / FILLED;
CANCEL_PENDING / EXPIRED_PENDING until terminal evidence; RECOVERY overlay on
uncertainty; TERMINAL keeps costs unresolved until accounting agrees. Existing
native_orders economic states remain backward-compatible. Dashboard/Guard health
surfaces unresolved recovery alerts; logs record transitions. Entries remain
blocked until native exposure, orders, actions and costs are reconciled.
