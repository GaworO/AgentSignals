Directional LIVE V1 — explicit closed-candle resting LIMIT policy

Scope and authorization (2026-10-25)
The user selected TradingView closed candles + Railway + CrossTrade cloud
Tradovate, maximum 1 MNQ, original Directional LONG/SHORT, structural SL and
exactly 2R gross at the approved original LIMIT. A/B stays shadow. No new
strategy, NO-FILL recovery, subscription, runner or advanced manager writes.
The user accepted the controlled MFFU pilot based on historical broker
protection plus deterministic recovery tests. These tests are not represented
as independent broker verification. Financial submission remains unarmed.

Policy selection
DIRECTIONAL_EXECUTION_POLICY_FILE=/app/directional_resting_limit_policy.json
execution_model=RESTING_LIMIT_CLOSED_M1_V1
Absent or unknown policy blocks. Existing EXECUTABLE_BBO_V1 behavior remains
unchanged: it never falls back to candles automatically. Closed M1 observations
have no bid, ask, spread or executable price. Unsupported NT8 polling is skipped
only while the explicitly selected resting policy is valid.

Admission
- Exact contract/account and quantity 1; no simultaneous positions or scale-in.
- Original tick-valid LIMIT and structural SL; target = entry +/-2*SL distance.
- Closed immutable authenticated M1, last complete minute, maximum age 75s.
  Source timestamps and receipt time retained; no incomplete/future bars.
- Original BOS evidence and complete post-setup bars; touched bracket, changed
  structure/liquidity, changed geometry and expired setup remain blocking.
- Setup age <=120s, exact setup-specific unexpired genuine AI ALLOW, existing
  12/day 3/7/2 budget. WAIT/REJECT/missing/timeout do not dispatch.
- Fresh account and thesis <=3s, existing Guard/news/session/one-loss/day rules.
- Missing account rules/coverage/margin still blocks. The policy never invents
  these values and does not substitute a balance for an account allowance.
- LIMIT/GTD deadline=min(original setup, AI validity, cancelAfter,
  dispatch time +600s). Final delayed validation cannot outlive this deadline.

Cost-inclusive sizing
LONG distance=entry-SL; SHORT distance=SL-entry; per-contract stop risk=2*distance.
Add configured RT cost estimate $2.24 and proposed/accepted 2 ticks x$0.50=$1
stop-slippage allowance. This assumption is not calibrated and is not a bound
on losses or slippage. Floor quantity by the tightest strategy risk budget,
remaining drawdown after reserve, daily allowance, known margin and contract
capacity, then cap at 1. If even 1 exceeds risk, quantity=0 and reject.
Do not tighten/loosen the stop to fit size. Original sizing is logged.

After a confirmed better fill
Keep original absolute structural stop and target. Persist actual fill distance,
gross/net estimated risk and remaining quantity using the cost assumptions
frozen at dispatch. Actual gross RR may exceed 2 because the approved original
target does not move. Actual realized fees remain reconciled separately; an
estimate is not an actual charge. A fill beyond the SL or worse than the LIMIT
uses the existing owned emergency recovery, never a replacement bracket.

Residual risks explicitly disclosed
Unknown live spread and immediate marketability; a LIMIT can fill immediately.
Intrabar invalidation and adverse selection before the next closed candle.
Cancel/fill races, delayed cancellation and fills after requested expiry.
Gap and stop-market slippage can exceed the assumed allowance and headroom.
Broker/provider disconnection can delay observation and recovery. CrossTrade's
demo/prop GTD watcher is not a hard exchange-enforced expiration guarantee.

Activation and rollback
No arming permission is changed by this release. V3_ENTRY_ARMED remains0.
Before user activation: fresh account rules/coverage/margin, flat account/no
working orders/unresolved intents, current feed/session and all Guard gates.
A new setup still needs its own actual unexpired ALLOW. Never preapprove one.
User-only final switch: V3_ENTRY_ARMED=1 after every required gate passes.
Emergency entry stop: EXEC_KILL_SWITCH=true and V3_ENTRY_ARMED=0. Keep
LIVE_EXECUTION_ENABLED=true for existing owned protective recovery; retain
native SL/TP/OCO. Reconcile/cancel owned pending entries and confirm outcomes.
Rollback code only while flat/reconciled with a fresh backup, preserving journals.
Do not deploy obsolete ancestor d0d2c83 without the resting lifecycle fixes.
