Critical numerical incompatibility
A fixed gross 2R bracket cannot meet net >=2R with positive costs. With gross
stop loss L, target reward G, round-trip cost C and stop slippage stress S:
  net RR = (G-C)/(L+C+S)
  net RR >=2 requires G >=2L+3C+2S.
The code rejects this conflict, does not silently extend TP or assume a better
entry fill. The synthetic integration example uses a 3R protocol bracket; that
is not validation/activation of the production 3R runner. Production original
fixed 2R remains unchanged. A separately approved and validated bracket policy
is needed to satisfy the newly requested net criterion.
