TANJA COMPLETE SIMULATED LIFECYCLE — 10 October 2026

IMPLEMENTED LOCALLY
paper_lifecycle.PaperLifecycle integrates the existing observer plan schema,
management_policy local/ES partial and add rules, and management_review stop
rules into a durable SQLite event-driven simulator. It has no network client,
API credential access, broker adapter, production worker or order route.
Railway remains unchanged: 2.10-core-management-observe, observation-only.

INPUTS AND CAUSALITY
- Supply a current frozen PLAN_READY observer decision with AI, news and account
  gates passed, plus an explicitly sourced, already-known local objective.
- Entry is restricted to the confirmed M5 immediate entry policy in either direction.
- Supply chronological tick-aligned quote events, not inferred OHLC paths.
- A request and its acknowledgement/fill require different, later quote events.
- Future/unreceived candles cannot confirm swings or ES session extremes.
- SQLite transactions save state and event results together. Identical event IDs
  replay their saved result; conflicting retries and backward time are rejected.
- Restart reloads pending requests, lots, stop, target, quantity and partial/add flags.

LIFECYCLE
1. Reserve a simulated entry using the selected stop/target and cost-inclusive size.
2. A later quote models fill plus protective bracket confirmation atomically.
3. Existing stop or final target takes precedence over all later requests. A stop
   uses adverse slippage from the supplied quote; target limit fills at its price.
4. Pending management must be acknowledged, rejected or explicitly held. While
   held, no additional management request is generated and existing protection stays.
5. Stop tightening has the first available slot (including pre-news protection).
   Then local partial, ES partial and add-on serialize, one request at a time.
6. Partial/add fills resize simulated protection to the confirmed remaining size.
7. An exit cancels pending requests and remaining simulated protection. No reversal.

SIZE AND ACCOUNTING
- Per-trade budget is the lesser of the supplied plan and reported account allowance.
- Initial contract floor includes both fees plus configured entry/stop slippage.
  Fill-price deterioration causing a budget overrun rejects the entry.
- Original simulated entry quantity is the immutable 20% base, rounded down.
- Add fills may reduce further or reject for risk, capacity, expiry or geometry.
  All lots share the existing stop and the initial dollar budget. Realized/unrealized
  profits provide no extra risk allowance. FIFO accounts for lot exits and fees.
- Add capacity reserves the simulated position in addition to reported exposure;
  uncertain overlap can conservatively under-size it.
- News protection uses original base-entry fill minus/plus 1.5 points after an add,
  not a weighted average. This is an explicit simulation convention, not evidence
  of Tanja's add-on behaviour. Stop never loosens.
- Pending risk-increasing entry/add requests cancel on a supplied known news
  blackout. The upstream news/calendar guard must still validate new decisions.

MODEL LIMITS
The 'fill' acknowledgement means a full synthetic fill at the next supplied quote.
The 'hold' and 'reject' options exercise unresolved/rejected requests. Bracket
replacement and cancellation are atomic simulator assumptions. Partial broker
fills, exchange order queues, lost connections, cancellation/fill races and actual
broker position reconciliation are not represented. Never use this simulator's
position state as confirmation of a real position.

The simulator is not a historical M1 backtester. Sparse quote events miss intrabar
stops/targets. The example charts connect supplied quotes only for illustration.
No outcome measures from these synthetic examples establish profitability or 1:1
fidelity. Local-objective choice, setup preference and initial-stop/target choices
still need comparison with additional Tanja recordings and no-trade sessions.
