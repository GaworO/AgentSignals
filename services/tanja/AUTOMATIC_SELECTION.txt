TANJA AUTOMATIC LONG RESEARCH v1 — 9 October 2026

Status
Automatic selection is implemented for two long momentum research recipes. No
manual setup, candle-strength, stop-anchor or target annotation is required.
This is a testable interpretation, NOT verified 1:1 replication or a validated
profitable strategy. No new AI requests or broker-order transport are present.

Hypothesis and source evidence
A liquidity excursion followed by a strong closing inversion and high break may
support immediate continuation toward opposing liquidity. Tanja discusses a
selected M5 inversion/high break/strong candle and no retracement on May 22,
video 01:19:30–01:25:47. Initial structural invalidation there differs from later
swing trailing. May 26, 00:49:04–00:50:04, describes a negative-one false-move
projection. July 17, 00:34:39–00:37:29, describes reclaim after monthly sellside
and an intended new high-of-day target. Original excerpts/hashes are retained in
../research_tanja_entry_rules_20261009/evidence.json.

These examples motivate conditional recipes, not the numeric defaults below.
The current automatic liquidity catalogue has completed overnight/prior-RTH
extremes, not a verified prior-month/week/ATH catalogue. The July case therefore
motivates the family but is NOT claimed reproduced by the current catalogue.

Inputs and clocks
Closed standard M1 ES, NQ and MNQ OHLC, UTC seconds; optional received_at.
Missing minutes are never filled. All three markets must reach the cutoff.
Decision receipt time is frozen; late arrivals cannot rewrite stored decisions.
Only 09:30–11:00 NY weekday research decisions; first cutoff is after 09:30.
This window and 15-second freshness cap are engineering limits, not Tanja rules.
Historical files without receipt times assume availability at candle close.
Continuous-contract adjustment/roll consistency remains to be independently
verified. NQ levels are never copied as MNQ prices.

Automatic setup selection
1. Reclaimed overnight/prior-RTH low after a strict sweep in the current RTH,
   with the deepest sweep no more than 30 minutes old; OR
2. Return to a bullish H1/H4 gap formed before RTH, close back above the zone,
   and paired ES/NQ divergence against the same completed reference window.
   A subsequent M1 close below the lower boundary invalidates the zone.
SMT is conditional for recipe 2; it is not mandatory for every reclaim.
Equal ES reference/current lows do not establish positive divergence.
The bullish-thesis classification is a measured recipe proxy, not independent
AI understanding of every higher-timeframe narrative.

Confirmation proxy
Three-candle bearish wick gap from the same excursion, first close above it,
plus close above the latest already-confirmed pre-sweep high. Candidate TFs:
5, 3, 2, 1 minute. Strict one-left/one-right swing convention; the right candle
must already have closed. Missing bars invalidate an interval.
Strong bullish candle: body/range >= 0.60, close in upper 25%, total range >=
median of previous 5 same-TF ranges. The gap's final-candle low must be in the
lower 35% of the available pre-trigger RTH range (up to 30 minutes).
All these numbers and conventions are provisional, versioned hypotheses.
No threshold optimization or claim of trader validation has occurred.

Automatic stop and target
Initial stop: lowest actual MNQ low in the paired reaction-to-trigger interval,
minus one tick. The reaction and one-tick buffer are explicit initial-invalidation
proxies; not every original Tanja initial stop used this exact anchor.
Target: nearest still-untouched candidate above entry among a completed-session
high, confirmed swing high or opposing HTF gap boundary. Selection uses NQ
context and independently calculated MNQ levels on matching intervals. The
nearest-obstacle preference is a research choice, not a proven target hierarchy.
If no such target is available, use a negative-one false-move projection from
the matched MNQ pre-sweep swing candle to the reaction low. Negative-half is
configurable. Off-tick projections are rejected rather than silently rounded.
No universal fixed-R target, ATH fabrication or front-running offset.

Ranking and abstention
Rank H4 POI, H1 POI, prior-RTH reclaim, overnight reclaim; then TF order above;
then latest gap. Log alternatives and measurements. Missing context, invalid
geometry, weak proxy confirmation or insufficient budget cause abstention.
Ranking is not optimized on outcome labels and is not verified Tanja ranking.

Sizing and risk
Explicit hypothetical price-risk budget and contract cap. Size is the integer
floor of budget / (MNQ point distance x $2), capped by the configured maximum.
Railway observer defaults: $100 price risk, maximum 1 MNQ. These are comparison
settings, NOT approved Builder risk limits. Local paper simulator rechecks
actual modeled fill risk including costs. The observer emits plans only.
No daily/weekly account drawdown checks or broker reconciliation exist here;
therefore executable orders are always disabled. One active position is enforced
only by the separate local paper simulator.

Exits and management
Compiler connects to the existing local long-only paper lifecycle. Existing
protection is checked before management on each supplied quote. Target/stop
fills, partials and bracket resizing are modeled, not broker acknowledgements.
User-selected news stop at entry minus 1.5 points and confirmed M1 swing trailing
remain separate management rules. No automatic universal partial quantity.
No new time-stop, discretionary early-exit rule, short mirror or 30-second
execution is invented. Retracement execution remains unsupported.

Service operation
selection_observer.py independently reads bars/collection_bars, not legacy AI
jobs. It runs every two seconds, processes the latest synchronized closed minute
once per policy revision, records frozen audits, and bounds retention to 100
research decisions (latest 30 shown). Missed minutes after restart do not become
fresh entries. Old ES/MNQ AI reviews and their API budget remain unchanged.
Endpoints /api/automatic-selection and /api/automatic-selection/audit/<revision>/<cutoff>
use the existing dashboard authentication and accept GET only.
TANJA_AUTO_SELECTION_ENABLED=true by default, false disables this observer.
TANJA_AUTO_PAPER_RISK_USD=100; TANJA_AUTO_PAPER_MAX_CONTRACTS=1.
No production environment variables need changing for observation-only use.

Validation and remaining scope
78 local engine/lifecycle checks and 78 service checks passed. New selector
checks cover automatic choices, complete intervals, future invariance, late
receipts, independent MNQ pricing, conditional SMT, HTF ranking, projections,
and no-plan cases. One synthetic end-to-end test reaches the paper target from
raw bars without manual choices. Desktop/mobile dashboard checks passed.
These are correctness checks, NOT 156 independent trades, a historical strategy
backtest or an out-of-sample validation. May examples are development evidence.
No win rate, profit factor, fidelity percentage or live-readiness claim exists.

Next evaluation: freeze policy before collecting forward decisions; compare
matched Tanja entries AND no-trade days, chosen TF/POI, initial stops and targets.
Evaluate disagreements without tuning on the same held-out cases. Numerical
