AI-TO-ENTRY INTEGRATION — 10 October 2026

Observation-only. No model calls or broker transport are added.

The ES/MNQ AI interpretation is a directional thesis gate on the independent
ES/NQ mechanical selector. It does not replace that selector's POI, confirmation,
stop or target selection. MNQ anchors remain MNQ prices. This cross-market
research arrangement and its numerical policies are not verified Tanja fidelity.

Latest attempt is read in the same database snapshot as the candles. A running,
failed, rejected, waiting or abstaining latest review cannot revive older approval.
A candidate must pass the original evidence validator, have complete context and
plan fields, and supply an immediate-confirmation entry and invalidation anchor.

The answer must already exist when the evaluated minute closes. No retrospective
approval of an earlier candle. Context expires 900 seconds after its SOURCE market
cutoff, not answer receipt; no previous-session or excluded-calendar-day reuse.

Every MNQ minute since source cutoff must be present and received by the decision
freeze. Any wick touching the tighter of the explicit invalidation and proposed
stop revokes context; any proposed target being touched also ends its usefulness
for new entries. Recovery does not resurrect that review: its entire monitoring
interval is checked again. A new validated review is required.

Only a same-direction mechanical PLAN_READY can remain PLAN_READY. Otherwise the
record is AI_CONTEXT_BLOCKED and its compiled plan is removed. Evidence and
mechanical candidate measurements remain available for review. Allowed plan
expiry is capped at the earlier mechanical-plan or AI-context expiry.

Every record freezes AI run ID, source time, answer time, expiry, direction,
invalidation witness and reasons. Late AI responses cannot rewrite existing rows.
Older rows retain their original policy revision and do not become AI approvals.

No open-position management, fills, drawdown controls or actual trading is added.
The six-call limit and holiday/weekend API guards remain unchanged. Fifteen-minute
context lifetime is a conservative engineering default, not empirically optimized.
