Agent50k operator-controlled activation — 2026-10-26

This release does not arm orders, enable recovery, or certify missing evidence.
The intended settings are original Directional LONG/SHORT, exact ICT AI review,
0.5% current equity risk, original structural SL, fixed 2R gross, LIMIT/GTD,
A/B shadow, and no discretionary management writes. No fixed 1-MNQ sizing cap.

What is implemented and tested
Original scanner -> exact setup request -> shared HYBRID AI journal -> immutable
ALLOW -> post-response context/expiry validation -> current account sizing/Guard
-> CrossTrade LIMIT/native OSO -> ACK separate from fill -> per-fill stop/target/
OCO verification -> closure/cost reconciliation. Account, policy, broker evidence
and entry switches all remain independently required. ALLOW never bypasses them.

Recovery registers only eligible original native intents while its flag is on;
it does not resurrect arbitrary historical BLOCKED/NO_FILL rows. At minute 10,
confirm broker cancellation, zero fills/positions/working orders, and settlement
before reserving one review. Original setup reviews have priority. FIBO_50 uses
old impulse anchors without the entry offset. DEEP_FVG uses 50% of a confirmed
gap and its first candle's wick; NEW_BOS_FVG uses an eligible original-scanner
BOS/FVG and the new impulse's structural swing. Unavailable evidence is UNKNOWN.
AI chooses a deterministic plan or WAIT/REJECT/CANCEL; it cannot edit geometry.
One replacement maximum; hard deadline 20 minutes from original activation.
Timeout, changed structure/geometry, expiry, unknown cancellation, a late fill,
or lost reconciliation prevents replacement. Restart never retries an uncertain
AI call or submission. A late fill is reconciled as exposure, not as NO_FILL.

Review scheduling: one initial CONTEXT attempt per allowed PREM/NYAM/NYPM session,
on the first eligible fresh packet; no hourly or material-event context calls.
Original setup and confirmed-NO_FILL Recovery requests have priority. Failed
opening attempts consume their reservation and are not retried in that session,
including after restart. SKIP_SESSIONS and MONDAY_MODE are respected; the current
MONDAY_MODE=nyam still excludes Monday PREM. PM_AH supports requested reviews only
when allowed; it does not receive a fourth opening-context call. Session-end
expiry applies to original and recovery reviews. Old missed setups are not replayed.

Budget: shared SQLite, 12/day America/New_York, fixed CONTEXT=3 / SETUP=7 /
POSITION=2, no transfers. Recovery consumes SETUP capacity, not another budget.
Reservations/unknown responses consume capacity; unknown external usage blocks.
A recovery review is not guaranteed if the seven setup slots are exhausted.
No paid AI or real broker writes are used by the execution-critical test suite.

Risk calculation
budget = floor_to_cents(verified current broker equity * 0.005)
per_MNQ = abs(original entry - structural SL)*2 + round-trip costs + stop allowance
quantity = floor(min(all risk/capacity allowances)/per_MNQ), further constrained
by verified contract/margin caps, max_quantity, exposure and broker scope.
Zero quantity rejects. LONG TP = entry+2*(entry-SL); SHORT TP = entry-2*(SL-entry).
The structural stop is not moved to fit quantity. Better fills do not loosen it.
Current estimate: round trip $2.24 + two MNQ ticks ($1) stop allowance, not a loss
bound. At equity $48,721.05: budget $243.60; 30-point SL costs $63.24/MNQ and
permits at most 3 under this budget alone. Actual quantity requires all gates.
Floor $48,161.90 means $559.15 gross headroom/$459.15 after $100 reserve.
Provider NONE daily limit does not disable local DAY_LOSS_USD=550 or DAY_LOSS_N=1.

Mandatory evidence before activation (do not fabricate these fields)
1. mffu_account_evidence.json: account_binding, provider=MFFU, source_reference,
   observed_at_ms and expires_at_ms must describe actual dated account rules.
   Floor 48161.90, max_micro_contracts=40, daily_loss_policy=NONE are user-confirmed.
   verified=true is a claim about evidence, not a substitute for verification.
2. margin_policy: verified, source_reference, contract, observed_at_ms,
   expires_at_ms and mode=CONTRACT_CAP_ONLY only if that exact account rule is
   established; otherwise FIXED_PER_CONTRACT plus actual usd_per_contract.
   Zero used margin does not prove capacity or lack of margin requirements.
3. reconciliation_anchor: verified, flat=true, account_binding, source_reference,
   cash, at_ms (at/before current NY midnight), capture_complete_since_ms
   (at/before anchor), coverage_reference, consecutive_losses and, if nonzero,
   last_loss_closed_ms. Prove no omitted fills/fees/transfers since the anchor.
   Do not simply declare today's balance a complete historical checkpoint.
4. broker_workflow_verification: existing max_quantity=1 records the legacy
   evidence scope, NOT the desired sizing rule. With EXEC_MAX_QTY=40 the current
   scope validator requires verified protection evidence covering that cap:
   quantity_scope_verified, quantity_scope_reference and observed_protected_quantity
   >= max_quantity. Do not set observed quantity to 40 based on an account limit.
   Historical A/B 5-MNQ OCO does not prove Directional/recovery at 40. This is an
   explicit remaining technical acceptance restriction, not missing user consent.
5. Approved candle policy: RESTING_LIMIT_CLOSED_M1_V1, correct account/contract,
   original LIMIT, original geometry and gross2R, dated validation references.
   Unknown spread, immediate executable price, intrabar movement, delayed cancel,
   late fills, stop slippage and disconnect risks remain. Current accepted limits:
   closed M1 <=75s; signal <=120s; account/thesis <=3s; initial pending <=600s.
   These limits are configured assumptions, not statistically calibrated BBO bounds.

Emergency stop and rollback (operator actions)
Set EXEC_KILL_SWITCH=true and V3_ENTRY_ARMED=0; deploy, confirm effective values.
Railway deployment latency means this is NOT an instantaneous broker cancel.
Check the broker directly for owned pending entries and fills during the change.
Cancel only identified unfilled ENTRY parents if needed; reconcile cancel/late
fill before doing anything else. Never blanket Cancel All or delete SL/TP children.
Keep LIVE_EXECUTION_ENABLED=true, broker sync, native transport and persistent
journals running so protective recovery remains available. Existing confirmed
native SL/TP/OCO remain broker-side; protection already missing cannot be repaired
by a configuration switch during a provider outage. Inspect it at the broker.
After no pending/unknown entries remain, recovery can be disabled by the operator.
A process restart cannot undo a fill. Prefer code rollback while flat/reconciled;
never restore an old DB over current fills/AI reservations. Use a known consolidated
release and preserved config paths, with entry kill still on. Do not reconnect
obsolete codex/directional-readiness-20261020. Revalidate runtime hashes, policies,
journals, broker state and all gates before reactivation.
