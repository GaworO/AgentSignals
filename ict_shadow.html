How these numbers are calculated

Costs are scanner-only USD estimates, before tax, with any pricing cushion included. Unresolved requests retain a separate reserve. Week starts Monday in New York; performance periods use the idea’s creation date.

Trades are independent shadow ideas, not broker orders or a joint portfolio. P&L and PF use completed, unambiguous candle simulations at one MNQ each. No-fill, rejected, invalidated, open and uncertain ideas are excluded from realized P&L. Net P&L is unavailable because fees and slippage are unverified. PF is unavailable without realized losses.

Simulations use one-tick fill-through and fixed initial exits. Intrabar order ambiguity and missing candles remain unknown. Free-form AI invalidation is not automatically simulated. The chart uses stored closed candles and is not executable market data.
