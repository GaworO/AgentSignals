Code and routing
----------------
agent.py: /bars -> isolated continuation_scan_runtime.py -> forward ledger
continuation_live.py -> Directional entry rules -> setup queue -> AI review
-> durable WAITING_AI -> single resume -> account Guard -> prepared native
order -> fresh account snapshot -> final execution gate -> CrossTrade OSO.
The final gate runs after durable order reservation, before POST. Failure is
logged and no POST occurs. Unknown submissions are never automatically retried.

execution_policy.py: V3_ONLY separates A/B and Continuation from Directional.
builder_crosstrade.py: V3_ONLY also checks the persisted strategy identity.
ab_v3_live.py: persists AB_DIRECTIONAL provenance with immutable initial bracket.
A/B shadow bookkeeping remains separate from account risk and realized P&L.
No retrospective orders are caught up at startup.

LIVE_EXECUTION_ENABLED defaults false. With false, entries, native financial
POSTs, manager exits and Guard cancel/flatten writes are disabled.
EXEC_KILL_SWITCH=true blocks new entries but allows protective operations
if the master write switch is already true. It does NOT itself flatten.
Do not disable protective writes while an unhandled live position exists.

execution_trace.py records signal observation, source bar/activation, review
start/completion, eligibility, POST, acknowledgment, observed fills and terminal
broker states. Existing bars table stores source timestamp and receipt time.
AI reviews and native journals retain their original timing evidence.
Trace write failure prevents a new order; it is not silently ignored.

AI and final validation
-----------------------
SETUP mode queues only eligible forward Directional setups in NYAM 09:30–11:00
and NYPM 13:30–16:00 America/New_York. Maximum 12 attempts per NY date, shared
with earlier stored usage; unknown responses consume an attempt and are not
retried automatically. Source Tanja AI must remain disabled.
Review includes causal multi-timeframe context, supplied liquidity evidence,
entry, SL, TP and 2R/3R alternatives. It cannot change those prices or quantity.
ALLOW is bound to one order, direction and exact geometry. WAIT/VETO, missing,
expired, changed or invalidated review blocks. The optional periodic filter
flag cannot bypass a required SETUP review.

execution_validation.py requires an approved documented policy, current bid/ask,
market/contract identity, original bracket and minimum 2R, age/entry zone/spread/
slippage/volatility checks, and fresh server-side thesis/account-risk evidence.
No approved policy or production quote/thesis adapter is registered.
The server-side risk adapter now re-reads the persisted forward dispatch,
prepared bracket, quantity/budget and fresh account snapshot, then calls the
actual Guard again. Only its exact, unsubmitted native and Guard reservations
are recognized; other reservations, costs, fills and loss/position limits still
block. Expiry, write interlocks and exit requests are rechecked after validation
so a slow read cannot authorize a canceled or expired entry.
Consequently this release intentionally cannot pass final execution validation,
even if someone changes the ordinary strategy switches. Completing those
adapters is a remaining engineering prerequisite, not an activation flag.
No candle close is substituted for bid/ask.

CrossTrade versus legacy adapter
-------------------------------
CrossTrade Tradovate is retained because the connected account and historical
native OSO fills are verified. Exact account/environment, dated MNQ contract,
0.25 tick, positive integer quantity, scalar protective levels, GTD, flat-only
and maxPositions=1 are enforced. Acknowledgment proves acceptance, not a fill.
Partial fills remain visible but block manager ownership until fully reconciled.
Restart recovery requires exact native IDs/client ID. Cancel/close is account-
and order-owned; unknown requests are not retried. Costs must reconcile.
The legacy HTTP path is blocked for Directional: its assumed-fill behavior is
not suitable for this release. It is not substituted for CrossTrade.

Initial automated OSO activation uses Modify/AtExecution in observed Tradovate
data. The adapter now recognizes only the narrow verified initial activation
shape, with matching ownership/geometry and no conflicting cancel/modification.
Guard latches unconfirmed entries so a late acceptance cannot resurrect them.
An unconfirmed entry remains unresolved until broker evidence establishes its
state; no fake closed position is created.

AI evaluation and proposed limits
---------------------------------
Recorded mixed-version Directional ledger: 30 closed trades, model PF 1.5407,
win rate 46.67%, net expectancy +0.3144R, max drawdown 3.9906R. Stored transaction
cost model is 1.75 points/trade. NYAM/NYPM subset: 7 closed, PF 1.3529, win rate
42.86%, expectancy +0.2146R, max drawdown 2.1195R. Not broker returns or an
out-of-sample validation of today's NO_DIB/BOS50/AI combination.

A: no-AI historical ledger, as above.
B: exact setup AI cohort has zero historical reviews. PF/expectancy/DD and
missed entries cannot be estimated honestly.
C: asynchronous observational AI has the same executions as A by definition;
identical ledger metrics are not an independent result.

Read-only analysis used 26546 archived M1 rows and 60 Directional orders.
16 NY-session setups include 7 recorded fills. At 50–54 seconds all seven
recorded first-fill bars begin later; at 60 seconds three are inside their fill
bar (intrabar ordering unknown). At 120 seconds three first-fill bars have
ended. This does not prove a later order could not fill on a retest.

The original strategy places RESTING limits. Demanding a tiny current-price
distance can reject valid resting setups and is a strategy change.
Derived constraint: do not reprice a fixed 2R entry adversely (zero adverse
repricing); keep the original limit, SL and TP. No order is chased.
Research grids, not production permissions: age 30/60/120s, distance
0/0.025/0.05/0.1R; bid/ask spread 1/2/4 ticks; quote age 250/500/1000ms.
Spread and realized slippage are not identifiable from M1. Capture bid/ask,
order-event and fill timestamps, then validate on independent dates with the
unchanged detector, causal volatility definition and actual costs. No positive
production tolerance was selected from unavailable evidence.
Existing 120s AI review TTL is an observation safeguard, not validated entry TTL.

Measured validated AI attempts: N=4, median 49.589s, p95 59.027s, range
47.226–60.645s. Other/diagnostic attempts lasted 54.377, 60.765 and 161.610s.
Fresh sequential broker read batch: 0.696s. These are different samples, not an
end-to-end latency percentile. Provider inference dominates observed wait;
queue-on-setup and callback resume avoid an extra periodic scan after completion.
No claim of faster model inference or improved PF is made.
