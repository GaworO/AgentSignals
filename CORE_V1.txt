TANJA CORE V1 — frozen implementation, 10 October 2026

This is a versioned research implementation of the user's selected rules.
It is not a verified reproduction of every Tanja trade or a live execution system.

CONFIRMED CORE
- Completed M5 close strictly beyond the selected inversion boundary and swing.
  A wick, unfinished M5, M1/M2/M3 trigger, or equality at the level cannot enter.
  Entire candle body beyond the level is NOT required.
- No numerical body/range, close-location, range-expansion or range-position veto.
  Measurements remain in the audit for diagnosis, not as entry requirements.
- Long and short directions; quantity floors from 0.5% reported current balance
  and stop distance, reduced by the separate reported account limits.
- First local partial: reached selected objective, floor(original contracts/5),
  skip below one, preserve a runner. News alone does not trigger this partial.
- Remaining position: confirmed M1 swing stop and final target; no independent
  wick-rejection exit. Stop only tightens. Pre-news: one minute before, adverse
  entry offset 1.5 points. Entry blackout: five minutes before/after.

IMPLEMENTATION CONVENTIONS — NOT VERIFIED TANJA RULES
- M1/M5 swing confirmation uses one strict neighbour on each side; right bar closed.
- H4/H1 gap + conditional SMT, prior-RTH reclaim, then overnight reclaim ranking.
- Initial stop outside paired MNQ reaction extreme by one tick.
- Final target nearest qualifying untouched objective, then false-move projection.
- Session 09:30–11:00 NY, 30-minute excursion/gap ages and freshness timeouts.
- Mechanical ES/NQ selection filtered by ES/MNQ AI context, not direct NQ AI analysis.

ADDITIONAL USER DECISIONS — 10 October 2026
- Separate ES partial: once per position, floor(20% of original contracts),
  preserving a runner. ES must set a new current New York RTH session high for
  longs, mirrored new session low for shorts. ATR alternative is explicitly OFF.
- ES detection uses a completely closed post-entry M1 bar against every earlier
  RTH M1 bar (09:30 NY start). Missing session history, delayed/unfinished bars,
  equal extremes and a partly pre-entry trigger bar do not qualify. This is a
  causal implementation with bar-close latency, not intrabar execution fidelity.
- Add-on: at most one per trade, floor(20% of original contracts), capped further
  by available shared risk and account contract capacity. Requires a fresh,
  same-direction gated M5 plan after original entry. An original entry cannot
  trigger its own add. News, AI and account checks must still pass.
- The add shares the existing stop and original risk budget; no second 0.5%
  budget and no credit for unrealized profits. A fill must be reconciled before
  adjusting bracket quantities or accepting another management action.
- Partial pending/done and add pending/done flags are required inputs. Each
  action has a stable per-position intent ID. The caller must serialize events:
  existing stop/final target first, pending requests reconciled, local partial,
  ES partial, then add. Two partials must not be sent concurrently.

STILL REQUIRES VALIDATION
The local partial function requires an explicit sourced objective. General
preferred-objective and initial-stop selection are still documented assumptions,
not a verified reconstruction of Tanja's discretion.

WHAT RUNS
Railway saves research entry decisions plus the management policy version.
management_policy local/ES partial and add-on functions, and management_review.propose_stop
are tested offline functions requiring supplied position and fresh quote state.
They do not run a broker position loop. No broker fill, cancellation, bracket
resize, synchronized balance or automatic order management is added here.

VALIDATION
Tests demonstrate software rules, causal bars and quote checks, not profitable
trades or Tanja fidelity. Original lower-timeframe fixtures remain rejection
regressions. Positive M5 fixtures are synthetic, not relabelled historical trades.
Compare this frozen policy on additional labelled entries AND no-trade sessions
before changing the conventions or claiming a 1:1 match.
